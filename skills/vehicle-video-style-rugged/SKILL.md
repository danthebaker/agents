Apply a **rugged / off-road** treatment. This vehicle sells on capability and
adventure (or real work).

- **Tone:** bold, confident, adventurous. Emphasise capability, toughness,
  go-anywhere ability, ground clearance, traction, presence.
- **Narration language:** strong, grounded, active. Capability details (4WD,
  clearance, towing, off-road kit) land; keep it authentic, not macho cliche.
- **Camera moves:** confident and purposeful — an assertive quarter orbit, a strong
  low push toward the front, a track along a muscular flank. Controlled, not shaky.
- **Feel:** a bold, adventurous advert; a strong voice.
