Apply a **heritage / luxury** treatment. This vehicle sells on craftsmanship,
presence, and provenance — not stats.

- **Tone:** refined, understated, quietly confident. Let the car speak; never gush.
  Reference materials, design lineage, presence, the sense of occasion.
- **Narration language:** unhurried, elegant sentences. No slang, hype, or
  exclamation. Words like "poise", "presence", "hand-finished", "timeless" fit when
  earned; never force them.
- **Camera moves:** slow and deliberate — a gentle glide along the flank, an
  unhurried push into the cabin, a slow reveal of a detail. Nothing snappy.
- **Feel:** a premium marque film. Calm, spacious, cinematic music; a measured voice.
- **Avoid:** fast cuts, energetic language, gadget-listing, anything that reads as a
  bargain-forecourt advert.
