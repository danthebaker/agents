Apply an **executive** treatment. This vehicle sells on refinement, technology,
and assured comfort.

- **Tone:** confident, polished, professional. Emphasise refinement, cabin tech,
  comfort, efficiency, quiet capability.
- **Narration language:** measured and articulate; assured without hype. Highlight
  standout tech and comfort where they matter to a professional buyer.
- **Camera moves:** smooth, composed, moderate-paced — a clean glide along the
  flank, a steady push across the dashboard and screens.
- **Feel:** a polished, corporate-premium film; an assured voice.
