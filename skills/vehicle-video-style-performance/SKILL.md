Apply a **performance** treatment. This vehicle sells on the thrill of driving.

- **Tone:** energetic, confident, enthusiast-savvy. Focus on pace, handling, grip,
  sound, the driver's connection to the car.
- **Narration language:** punchy, short, active sentences with drive. Concrete
  performance details land — but describe the experience, don't just list numbers.
- **Camera moves:** dynamic but CONTROLLED — a quick-but-smooth track along the
  profile, an assertive push toward the front, a sweeping quarter orbit. Energetic,
  never shaky or chaotic.
- **Feel:** a propulsive, driving-focused advert; a lively voice.
- **Audience:** often younger / enthusiast — a soft cue for energy, not a licence
  for stereotype or cliche.
